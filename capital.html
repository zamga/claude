The Liquidity Pivot: repricing cross-border risk in a higher-for-longer regime.

Our base case anticipates a measured normalization of deal velocity through the second half, as sponsors recalibrate to a structurally elevated cost of capital. We see selective re-opening in cross-border syndication — with strategic acquirers regaining primacy over financial buyers across industrials and the energy transition.
