# Uncovered: award audit

Scored the way the juries score: the site jury on design, usability, creativity and content (weighted 40/30/20/10), the developer jury on semantics, animation, accessibility, performance, responsiveness and markup. Scores are out of 10, measured where a measure exists.

## Site jury

| Criterion        | Score   | What earns it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | What loses points                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Design (40%)     | 8.6     | One idea carried everywhere: engraved seals, serials, microtext, footnotes, the A4 page. Bodoni Moda with optical sizing against Hanken Grotesk's tabular figures. Daylight and UV are designed as two looks of one document, not an inversion. | No imagery beyond the seals and charts; the coverage and pricing sections are conventional next to the rest. |
| Usability (30%)  | 8.8     | One field does the important thing and sits above the fold on a laptop. Contents and a source rail in the reader; footnotes unfold under their paragraph on a phone. Focus lands on the new page's heading after every navigation.              | The request page can show the research plan but cannot run it yet.                                           |
| Creativity (20%) | 8.5     | A seal engraved from every company's name, live as you type. Prose bound to the model. "What the price implies" in place of a rating. The UV inspection lamp.                                                                                   | The engraving is the only moment people will screenshot; there is no share card for a seal yet.              |
| Content (10%)    | 9.0     | A real, sourced initiation on a real company: 17 graded sources, eight exhibits, a computed DCF, dividend and multiple valuation, and a summary whose claims are tested against its numbers.                                                    | One sample report; 2025 figures are the company's unaudited release; one fact rests on Wikipedia.            |
| **Weighted**     | **8.7** |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |                                                                                                              |

## Developer jury

| Criterion       | Score | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Semantics       | 9.0   | Landmarks, one `h1` per page, numbered sections with labelled headings, real tables with headers (and explicit roles where phones restack them), figures with captions, charts with descriptions and hidden data tables.              |
| Animation       | 8.5   | Every motion is CSS on transform and opacity, specified in `docs/STORYBOARD.md`, gated by reduced motion and complete at rest. The seal engraving is incremental and starts only when visible.                                        |
| Accessibility   | 9.5   | axe WCAG 2.2 AA: no violations on five pages, two themes, desktop and phone. Lighthouse accessibility 100 on every page. Keyboard paths and focus management tested end to end.                                                       |
| Performance     | 8.5   | Lighthouse mobile 93–96, total blocking time under 90ms, layout shift 0 on every page; LCP 2.6–2.8s in the simulated slow-4G profile, over the 2.5s budget.                                                                           |
| Responsiveness  | 9.0   | Phones get their own patterns (per-act sheets, unfolding sources, a seal inside the company field), not a squeezed desktop. No horizontal scroll at 390px; tables scroll inside their own regions with shadows and pinned row labels. |
| Markup and meta | 8.5   | Prerendered HTML for every page with its own title, description, schema.org data and, when the address is configured, canonical links and a sitemap. No social image yet.                                                             |

## Prioritised fixes

| #   | Fix                                                                                                                                                    | Why                                                                                                                                       | Impact                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Metric-matched fallback fonts (`size-adjust`, ascent and descent overrides) for Bodoni Moda and Hanken Grotesk, and a smaller first-screen font subset | The largest paint repaints when the web fonts arrive; matched fallbacks keep its size, so LCP lands at first paint (about 1.8s simulated) | Performance to 97+, LCP within budget |
| 2   | Social cards: render each company's seal, name and fair-value range to an image at build time and per report                                           | Every report becomes a shareable object, which is the creative jury's "signature moment" travelling off-site                              | Creativity, reach                     |
| 3   | The research engine (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`)                                                                                                       | The request page currently stops at the plan                                                                                              | Usability, the product itself         |
| 4   | A second and third sample: a private Slovenian company from AJPES filings, and an EU listed company from its single-electronic-format filing           | Proves "every company", not only a listed one                                                                                             | Content, trust                        |
| 5   | Reconcile Krka's 2025 figures with the audited annual report and replace the Wikipedia citation with the company's own pages                           | Removes the weakest evidence in the sample                                                                                                | Content                               |

## Verdict

**Iterate, then ship.** The design and the document are award-level and the build is production-grade (tested, accessible, prerendered, fast). Ship the front page and sample report after fix 1; fix 2 belongs in the launch; the engine is the next phase.
