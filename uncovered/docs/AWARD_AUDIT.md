# Uncovered: award audit

Scored the way the juries score: the site jury on design, usability, creativity and content (weighted 40/30/20/10), the developer jury on semantics, animation, accessibility, performance, responsiveness and markup. Scores are out of 10, measured where a measure exists. This audit covers the site after the "Held to the light" redesign, and the research engine.

## What a technical jury would ask of the engine

| Question                           | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can the model invent a number?     | Not one that reaches a report. A figure is recorded only if its number, as printed, sits inside a quotation that occurs word for word in the document the engine read. The gate then checks every number in every sentence against the passages that sentence cites, and withholds what it cannot trace. |
| Can a document steer the engine?   | It reads only through the engine's tools, and fetches only addresses that appeared in search results, in the request or in a page already read, so it cannot put anything of its own into an address. Public addresses only, checked on the connection itself; robots.txt honoured.                      |
| Who can spend money, and how much? | Access codes, runs per address per hour, runs at once and a time limit per run. Every run keeps an audit of documents, quotations, tokens, searches and cost.                                                                                                                                            |
| Has it run for real?               | Not yet. It is tested end to end with a scripted model client and a test filing, through the API, the progress stream and the browser. The first live runs are fix 1.                                                                                                                                    |

## Verdict

**Iterate, then ship.** The design is at the level the juries reward: one idea, made physical, carried from the hero to the register, accessible and resting when motion is reduced. The engine is built and tested, and the gate is what makes it safe to run: nothing reaches a reader without a quotation behind it. But the engine has not yet met a real filing. Open it to a closed beta (access codes) after fix 1; fixes 2 and 3 are what separate a Site of the Day from a Site of the Year, and belong in the public launch with fix 4.
