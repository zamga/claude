# Uncovered: storyboard

The front page reads as one argument: anyone can be covered (hero), almost nobody is (gap), here is how (four acts), here is what you get (anatomy), every number is sourced (provenance), the value is a range with the price on it (valuation), where it works (coverage), what it costs (pricing), why to trust it (principles). The sample report, the request page and the method page carry the same document language.

Motion values below are the shipped CSS. Every animation is gated by `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and rests in its final state.

## Sample report

Contents on the left (a sticky bar of chips on narrower screens), the report in the middle, the source rail on the right. Sections: Cover, Investment summary, The business, Markets, Financial performance, Ownership and capital returns, Valuation, Risks and catalysts, Sources, Disclosures. Eight numbered exhibits.

- Reading progress: a 2px gradient rule under the masthead, driven by the scroll timeline where supported (no script).
- Contents: the current section is the last one whose top has passed the upper third of the screen; its rule engraves in, 560ms.
- Footnotes: hover or focus shows the source in the rail with how often it is cited; a tap on a phone unfolds it under the paragraph, 420ms.
- Arriving at an anchor (for example from a page miniature on the front page) scrolls to the section and moves focus to it.
- Print: the report alone on A4, one section to a page, colours kept.

## Request

A three-part form (the company; filings; your question) beside the pending cover, whose seal engraves as the name is typed (on phones, a small seal engraves inside the company field). Where the server runs no engine, submitting turns the request into a coverage slip with a serial, the seal, and the research plan the engine would run, each step marked Ready or Needs you; the slip rises in, 700ms `--ease-out`, and a note says plainly that nothing is sent or stored.

Where the engine runs, the form asks for an access code if the server needs one, and submitting sends the request with its files. The address becomes `/initiate?run=<id>`, so the run can be left and returned to (opened again, the page arrives showing the run as it stands, then carries on live), and the slip becomes the record of the research as it happens:

| Moment              | What moves                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The run starts      | The slip rises in, 700ms `--ease-out`. Its seal starts blank and engraves its first lines, 1,600ms `--ease-engrave`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Research            | Each verified passage and document advances the seal a little further, up to its lace (about half of it), each step 1,600ms `--ease-engrave`. The counts change as the evidence is verified. The working step's top rule engraves left to right, 2.4s `--ease-engrave`, repeating, and the live line pulses (a ring spreading from the dot, 1.6s). |
| Model, write, check | Each stage moves the seal on, to its weave, its rosette and its ring. Steps turn from Waiting to Working to Done; the working notes add a line at the top for each thing the desk does, such as a draft sent back by the checks.                                                                                                                   |
| Covered             | The seal completes. 1.4s later the Covered stamp is struck over it: it falls from 1.6 times its size to 0.96 and settles, 520ms `--ease-out`. The slip's rule turns intaglio and the way to the report appears.                                                                                                                                    |
| Stopped             | A note in the reported-grade colour says why, with the way to edit the request or start again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

With reduced motion the seal is drawn at once to where the run stands, the stamp appears without striking, and nothing pulses. A polite live region announces each stage ("Building the model.") and the end ("The initiation is ready."); the counts and notes are not announced, so a screen reader is not flooded.
