02 Approach

We are deliberately slow where others are fast.

Conviction is expensive. It demands research that outlasts the quarter, and the patience to wait for price to meet value.

Every mandate begins with a single question — what could permanently impair this capital? — and only proceeds when the answer is structurally bounded. We size positions to survive being wrong, then concentrate where we are demonstrably right.

Our partners are pensions, endowments, and sovereign funds who measure success in decades. That horizon is our advantage: it lets us underwrite volatility as opportunity rather than threat, and to hold assets long enough for compounding to do the quiet work.

  • → Downside first. We model the loss before the gain.
  • → Alignment, not assets. We co-invest in every fund.
  • → Transparency by default. Quarterly attribution, fully unpacked.
