Legal frame

Why no prospectus is required.

Offers of securities to the public with a total consideration in the Union of less than €12,000,000, calculated over a period of 12 months, are exempt from the obligation to publish a prospectus. Regulation (EU) 2017/1129, as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/2809 (EU Listing Act). Applies from 5 June 2026.
